What the PGA Tour told Will Zalatoris about his potential return as he announces season-ending surgery

The latest chapter of the heartbreaking story of Will Zalatoris has been written.

The 30-year-old announced he’ll undergo yet another back surgery, which will keep him sidelined for all of 2027. This will be his third back operation in the last four years.

Zalatoris once looked like golf’s next superstar. Between 2020 and 2024, he posted seven top-10 finishes in 10 major championships, despite withdrawing from the 2021 Open Championship with an injury. He finished runner-up at three majors, falling one stroke short twice, and losing to Justin Thomas in a playoff at the 2022 PGA Championship.

Zalatoris played only four times on the PGA Tour this season and missed the cut twice. But the tour has given him some assurance about how he fits into its new 2028 schedule.

What the PGA Tour told Will Zalatoris about where he can play in 2028

The PGA Tour is introducing a brand new schedule for the 2028 season. It will feature far fewer events, all of equal value in prize money and FedEx Cup points, and two tiers with relegation and promotion between them.

The details of which players will fit into which tier are yet to be confirmed, although we do have some detail on which players qualify for the top tier, called the “Championship Series”.

Despite being a former PGA Tour winner, Zalatoris has been told he’ll compete on the second track, the “Challenger Series”, in 2028.

Speaking to Flushing It, he said, “I’ve been in contact with the Tour, which has been great. It looks like I can come back in 2028 and play in the Challenger Series if everything works like it should.”

MORE WILL ZALATORIS STORIES

Zalatoris still has a home on the PGA Tour, but if this surgery is successful, he’ll still have to earn his way back onto the biggest events on the calendar.

He said he has no expectations for the surgery and is taking it day by day. He continued, “It’s all connected together, and I just want to get it fixed. I can’t promise anybody anything because of what I’ve been through. I’m the oldest 30-year-old you will ever meet.”

Hopefully he can get healthy and return to top form, as golf was better when he was in contention.
