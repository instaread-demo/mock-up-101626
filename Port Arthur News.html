County Clerk Gives Info on Rain Damaged Mail Ballots

Published 12:59 pm Tuesday, October 13, 2026

By PA News

BEAUMONT — Jefferson County Clerk Roxanne Acosta-Hellberg has provided information regarding options for mail ballots for the Nov. 3 election.

“We have been notified of a concern regarding damage that occurred on delivery of mail ballots during a heavy rain in our area,” Acosta-Hellberg said. “Our office has a procedure in place to address this with voters.  We want to assure our voters that they have options.”

Option 1:  you can surrender the ballot at the polling location, we have several hundred that do this at every election. After surrendering the ballot the voter can vote on the machine.

Option 2:  We will gladly replace the damaged ballot. The ballot package must be physically returned to our office so that the ballot can be voided. A new ballot package will be mailed to the voter from our vendor.  2700+ ballots have been mailed, we have 3 that have followed option 2 and their new ballot package is being sent by the vendor.

For all mail ballot voters, mail ballots must be postmarked by Nov. 3 and received at our office by close of business Nov. 4.

We have already received 500+ mail ballots that are scheduled for review on Oct. 14 by the  Early Voting Ballot Board.  We are well ahead of schedule.

 As a reminder, every location offers curbside voting for those that are unable to enter our polling locations, instructions are posted at designated parking areas for curbside.

We look forward to serving you at all 11 early voting locations Oct. 19-30, 31 locations for Election Day.  Staff is trained and ready.  Any information you may need is always available on our site at www.jeffcotxvotes.gov or by calling the Elections Office at 409-835-8760.
