Looking for a cozy fall and winter pasta dinner? Something that’s filled with roasted and caramelized butternut squash, hearty Italian sausage and the freshness of wilted spinach.

To take it up a notch there’s a silky sauce made of cream, garlic and parmesan cheese which blankets all the other ingredients.

This pasta dish is truly simple to make, just roast the squash ahead of time and brown your sausage. I like picking a pasta shape that’s about a medium size, but the size is up to you and your liking.  This dish is perfect for a weeknight meal or even a special occasion.

There’s so much flavor in every bite, plus all the ingredients really compliment each other. It’s rustic and elegant all at the same time.

If you want to serve something along side this I would suggest a bright, crisp salad, something like an arugula salad with lemon and olive oil with some shaved parmesan on top, this would counteract the velvety richness of that luxurious  sauce.

Stay in and make this warm and cozy meal, you’ll be very happy.

Creamy Sausage And Spinach Butternut Squash Pasta
 
Print
A cozy fall and winter pasta dish filled with butternut squash, sausage and spinach, nestled in a decadent cream sauce.
Author: Marie
Recipe type: Pasta
Ingredients
  • 1 medium butternut squash, peeled and cubed into bite size chunks
  • 8 oz. pasta of your choice, preferably nothing long or too small
  • ½ to 1 lb. Italian sausage, out of the casing, spicy or mild, your choice.
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups of fresh spinach
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup or so of grated Parmigiano Reggiano (more for finishing and serving)
  • butter and olive oil
  • reserve pasta water
  • sage leaves for garnish
Instructions
  1. Roast the butternut squash tossed with olive oil, salt and pepper at 400F. Set aside
  2. In a large skillet, crumble the sausage and brown it to fully cooked. Remove the sausage, set aside and remove any excess oil.
  3. In same skillet add a knob of butter and some olive oil and let it melt.
  4. Boil your pasta al' dente, and save some pasta water.
  5. Toss in the garlic until golden then pour in the cream and let it simmer until you see bubbles along the edges of the pan.
  6. Next toss in the spinach so it can wilt.
  7. When cream starts to thicken add the sausage and the butternut squash.
  8. Lastly, add in your cooked and drained pasta to the mixture, tossing gently and adding a splash of pasta water if the mixture seems too thick.
  9. That's it, enjoy!
