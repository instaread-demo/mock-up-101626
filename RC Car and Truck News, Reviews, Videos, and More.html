Teaser – Cross RC 1/7 TR71 4WD Short Course Truck

Now being teased by Cross RC is the 1/7th scaled TR71 4WD Short Course Truck. The TR71 features a LCG chassis with four wheel independent suspension, to go along with a powerful brushless motor and 6S capable ESC. Here’s some highlights-

* Will be available as an RTR
* 3 colors to choose from
* Wheelbase – 426mm (16.7″)
* 7075 aluminum arm mounts
* 7075 aluminum shock towers
* Oil shocks with aluminum threaded bodies
* Drivetrain parts made from alloy steel
* 6S capable Hobbywing 150amp ESC
* Hobbywing 1650kV brushless motor
* 7075 4mm thick aluminum chassis
* Adjustable battery box
