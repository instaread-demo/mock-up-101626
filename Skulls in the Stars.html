More blogs to read?

Listen to audio version of this article

Hi all, just a quick note: I finally started regularly reading blogs on my WordPress Reader, and am endeavoring to check out what everyone is doing and like as much as I can! With that in mind, if there’s anyone following me who is writing a fun blog, particularly about science, fiction, or roleplaying, and I’m not following you yet, drop me a comment and let me know! I’ll do my best to follow along.

Time to bring back blogging! … cause social media is kinda terrible these days.

PS also if you have any recommendations please share!
