Results from Race 2 at the 2026 Italian WorldSBK, the second race of three at Cremona Circuit.

Iker Lecuona was victorious in Race 2 at the Italian WorldSBK. The Spaniard dominated once again to secure his first WorldSBK triple win and his fourth victory in total.

Nicolo Bulega finished second for the third time at Cremona, eight seconds behind his teammate but in position to take the title.

Yari Montella beat Alberto Surra to third by just over a second, while Alex Lowes completed the top-five.

Jake Dixon took his best result yet in sixth, ahead of Lorenzo Baldassarri, Garrett Gerloff, Axel Bassani, and Andrea Locatelli who rounded out the top-10.

Full WorldSBK results from Race 2 the Italian Round are below.

Superpole Race

Iker Lecuona won again in the Superpole Race at the Italian WorldSBK. Nicolo Bulega chose a softer tyre and was faster at the start, attacking on lap two. But Lecuona retaliated and held onto the lead, and by the midway point in the race the balance of grip shifted towards his harder SCX rear tyre.

Lecuona pulled away to the extent of two seconds by the end, but Bulega still took that second place to keep his title chances alive for Race 2.
